# How to Install UnGhost on Your iPhone

**Takes about 30 seconds. No App Store needed.**

---

1. 📱 Open **Safari** on your iPhone (must be Safari, not Chrome)

2. 🌐 Go to the UnGhost website URL

3. 📤 Tap the **Share** button at the bottom of the screen (the box with an arrow pointing up)

4. 📲 Scroll down and tap **"Add to Home Screen"**

5. ✏️ You can edit the name if you want — then tap **"Add"** in the top right

6. 🎉 UnGhost now appears on your home screen like a regular app!

---

**Tips:**
- The app works offline after you've opened it once
- Your data is saved on your device
- To uninstall, just press and hold the icon → Remove App (same as any app)
